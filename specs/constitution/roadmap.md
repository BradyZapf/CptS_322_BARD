# Roadmap

Process: Agile, in sprints with a Kanban board (GitHub Projects). Status of every story lives
in its GitHub issue and on the board, not in this file.

The order of work, as a product (the team picks what goes into each sprint):

1. **A working sync.** Connect a Canvas account, download a course and everything linked from it, say what was skipped and why. Then the first ways to read the archive: upcoming work in the user's timezone, search, erase.
2. **Text from files** (PDF and Word get a plain-text copy beside them), the full coverage report, and the local web page.
3. **Ways to use it with AI:** export a selection as one document, and the stdio MCP connector for desktop AI apps.
4. **Install on Windows, macOS and Linux, then polish.**

Icebox (maybe never): instructor features (several courses and sections), search by meaning,
OCR for scanned PDFs, text from PowerPoint and Excel.
